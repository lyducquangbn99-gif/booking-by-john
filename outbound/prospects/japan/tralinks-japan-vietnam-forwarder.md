## BYJ positioning
Do not pitch as if Tralinks lacks Vietnam coverage: it already has Vietnam operations and a Japan investor/network. Position BYJ as a shipment-specific Northern Vietnam/Hai Phong second option for:
- Hai Phong origin/destination benchmark;
- local trucking/customs second-check;
- overflow or difficult shipment support;
- alternate carrier/equipment/routing check when their normal setup is constrained.

## First-contact sequence
1. Email Ms Ha Nguyen and/or Mr Yoshida Akito using the public office contacts above.
2. Keep the opener narrow: ask whether they ever need a second operational/rate option for Hai Phong or Northern Vietnam shipments.
3. Ask for one live shipment to benchmark rather than sending a generic rate sheet.
4. For a live case collect: POL/POD or pickup/delivery, FCL/LCL/air, commodity/HS if available, equipment or CBM/weight, ready date, Incoterm, and current pain point.
5. Only then check live carrier/agent availability and quote. Do not claim current rates, space, transit time, or guaranteed savings without verification.
