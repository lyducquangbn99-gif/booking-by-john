## Execution sequence

1. Start with the Sales Department shared inbox, not a guessed personal address.
2. Keep the first message short: BYJ is a Vietnam-side freight option and can support a shipment-specific benchmark for Northern Vietnam / Hai Phong.
3. Ask whether their Japan desk ever uses an external Vietnam partner for overflow, local trucking/customs second-check, or ad-hoc shipment benchmarking.
4. If yes, request one live shipment only: origin/pickup, destination, FCL/LCL/air, commodity, weight/CBM, ready date and Incoterm.
5. Quote only after receiving live shipment details and checking current carrier/vendor conditions. Do not send stale blanket rates.
6. If no email response, use the published Sales Department phone and refer to the earlier email.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim Sinotrans lacks a Vietnam network or partner.
- Do not imply BYJ has handled Sinotrans cargo before.
- Do not promise rates, equipment, transit time or space before a live check.
- Do not treat public departmental emails as consent for bulk mailing; use one-to-one relevant outreach.
