## Why this prospect is qualified

Chikko Corporation is a Japanese freight forwarder/NVOCC with its own Vietnam subsidiary, CHIKKO VIETNAM CO., LTD. Chikko's current official international-transport page publishes a fixed LCL export service from **Yokohama to Hai Phong** (as well as Ho Chi Minh City and other Asian destinations). The same official page states that Chikko handles FCL/LCL NVOCC service to Vietnam and can coordinate port-to-port, dangerous-goods handling, warehousing, local documentation, customs clearance and door delivery.

This is direct route evidence, not an inferred Japan/Vietnam fit.

## BYJ angle

Do not send a generic "we are a freight forwarder in Vietnam" introduction. Chikko already has a Vietnam subsidiary, so BYJ needs a narrow complementary angle:

- Northern Vietnam / Hai Phong execution second-check when their own team needs overflow or alternative local handling.
- Competitive trucking/customs/port coordination for Hai Phong-origin or destination cargo.
- FCL/LCL rate benchmark for non-DG cargo and selected DG cases only after cargo details/SDS/UN class are known.
- Ask whether they ever need an external Vietnam-side backup for Northern Vietnam shipments rather than assuming they need a new agent.

## Suggested first-touch positioning

Reference their published Yokohama → Hai Phong consolidation service to show the outreach is route-specific. Ask one concrete question: whether they sometimes use external Vietnam-side support for Hai Phong pickup/delivery, customs, trucking, or overflow handling. If yes, request one real shipment (origin/destination, cargo, DG status if applicable, volume/weight, ready date, Incoterm) for a benchmark.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim Chikko is seeking a new Vietnam agent.
- Do not claim BYJ can handle DG cargo until the exact commodity, SDS, UN number/class, packing group and carrier acceptance are checked.
- Do not invent volumes, rates, schedules or customer names.
- Their Vietnam subsidiary means the outreach must be framed as backup/benchmark/overflow support, not replacement representation.
