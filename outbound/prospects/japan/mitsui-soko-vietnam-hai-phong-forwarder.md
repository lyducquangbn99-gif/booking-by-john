## BYJ positioning
Do not pitch as if Mitsui-Soko lacks Vietnam capability. Position BYJ Logistics as a responsive second Vietnam/Hai Phong option for a live shipment when their team wants:
- a freight/routing benchmark;
- an alternative carrier, equipment or sailing check;
- overflow or difficult-cargo support;
- a Vietnam-side trucking/customs second check.

## First-touch objective
Ask for one live/upcoming Vietnam shipment that they are willing to benchmark. Do not send an unqualified generic rate dump.

Suggested qualification before quoting:
1. Origin / destination
2. FCL, LCL or air
3. Commodity / HS code if available
4. Packages, gross weight and CBM
5. Container/equipment requirement if FCL
6. Cargo-ready date
7. Incoterm
8. Current routing/carrier constraint or pain point
