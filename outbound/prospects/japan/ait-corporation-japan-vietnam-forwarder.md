## BYJ positioning

Do NOT pitch as if AIT lacks a Vietnam office or Hai Phong capability. Their own network already covers Hai Phong.

Use BYJ Logistics as a shipment-specific second option when AIT needs:
- an independent Hai Phong ocean-freight benchmark;
- overflow / difficult-shipment support;
- an alternative equipment or sailing check;
- a second check for Vietnam-side trucking/customs/local execution.

## First-contact route

1. Start with the relevant Japan office switchboard for the shipment/customer geography; ask for the sales/operations person handling Vietnam / Hai Phong freight.
2. If operational routing is clearer from Vietnam, use the public Hai Phong branch number and ask for the person responsible for Japan lane coordination.
3. Ask for one live shipment to benchmark rather than sending a generic rate sheet.

## Qualification fields before quoting

- Japan origin/destination port
- Vietnam origin/destination and whether Hai Phong is required
- FCL/LCL/air
- commodity / HS code
- container type or CBM / gross weight
- ready date
- Incoterm
- current pain point: rate, space, equipment, transit, trucking, customs, or overflow

## Outreach angle

Short opener: BYJ Logistics can provide a second Hai Phong-side benchmark for a live Japan–Vietnam shipment when AIT needs an alternative rate/space/equipment or local-execution check. We are not proposing to replace AIT's existing Vietnam network; the goal is to be a practical backup option for shipment-specific cases.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim AIT currently has a shipment available.
- Do not claim BYJ has a better rate until a live shipment is checked.
- Do not imply AIT lacks Vietnam representation.
- Verify live carrier rate, equipment and sailing before any commercial quote.
