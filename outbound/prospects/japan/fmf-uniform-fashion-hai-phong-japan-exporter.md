## First-contact objective

Ask for one upcoming export shipment where FMF would accept a second freight benchmark, preferably Japan but also another international destination if that is the current requirement.

Qualify before quoting:

1. destination city/port/airport;
2. ocean FCL/LCL or air;
3. cartons/pallets, CBM and gross weight;
4. cargo description / HS code if available;
5. cargo-ready date;
6. Incoterm and pickup address;
7. delivery deadline or required transit;
8. current pain point: rate, space, cutoff, transit, trucking, customs or destination coordination.

## Suggested outreach angle

Lead with Hai Phong origin and a low-friction second-check offer: if FMF shares one real shipment, BYJ will check the current routing and return a shipment-specific benchmark rather than sending a generic rate sheet.

For Japan shipments, emphasize origin-side coordination from Hai Phong and only quote after live carrier/agent conditions are verified.

## Follow-up sequence

- Touch 1: email the public company inbox with the one-shipment benchmark proposition.
- Touch 2: if no response after a reasonable business interval, call the public office/PIC number and ask who manages export logistics.
- Touch 3: follow up only with route-specific value or a verified current option; avoid repetitive generic sales messages.
