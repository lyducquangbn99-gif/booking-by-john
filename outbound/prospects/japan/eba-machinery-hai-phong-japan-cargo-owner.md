## Why this is relevant to BYJ Logistics

EBA is a real industrial manufacturer in Hai Phong with a verified Japanese parent and EPE status. That makes it a relevant Northern Vietnam cargo-owner target for discovering recurring export/import flows involving Japan or other overseas destinations. The official sources do **not** establish that EBA currently has an open Japan shipment, so the first objective is shipment discovery rather than an unsolicited fixed-rate pitch.

## First-touch objective

Ask for one live or upcoming shipment that BYJ can benchmark. Keep the opener operational and short:

> We support international shipments from Hai Phong and would be glad to provide a second logistics option for EBA. If you have one upcoming import/export shipment, please share the origin/destination, cargo size/weight and ready date; we can check the current routing and freight option for comparison.

If the shipment is Japan-related, ask for the exact Japanese port/city before mentioning a specific lane.

## Positioning

- Offer BYJ as a shipment-specific **second option**, not as a claim that EBA lacks an incumbent forwarder.
- For Hai Phong origin, benchmark ocean freight plus Vietnam-side trucking/customs only after shipment facts are known.
- For Japan-parent flows, emphasize clear coordination and fast live-shipment checking rather than generic “best rate” language.
- If the real destination is not Japan, route the lead to the relevant BYJ commercial lane instead of forcing the Japan angle.

## Guardrails

Do not claim that EBA currently exports to Japan, has an active shipment, is dissatisfied with its current forwarder, or that BYJ will save a stated amount. Do not invent buyer names, shipment frequency, volumes, rates, space, equipment or transit times. Verify live commercial data before quoting.
