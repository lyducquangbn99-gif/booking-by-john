## Route-matched positioning

Do not pitch as a replacement for VIJACO's existing Japan/Vietnam capability. Position BYJ as a shipment-specific second option when VIJACO needs:

- an alternative ocean-freight benchmark from Hai Phong or HCMC;
- overflow / difficult-shipment support;
- an additional carrier/equipment option;
- a second check for Vietnam-side trucking/customs or destination-agent coordination.

## First-touch objective

Ask for one real upcoming shipment where a second freight option is useful. Before quoting, collect: POL/POD, FCL/LCL/air, commodity/HS, container/equipment or CBM/weight, ready date, Incoterm, target transit time and current pain point.
