## Why BYJ can be relevant
RK ENGINEERING is a strong fit for shipment-specific logistics benchmarking because the manufacturer itself confirms direct export capability from Hai Phong and Japan-bound cargo. BYJ should not pitch a generic forwarding partnership. The useful entry point is one live/upcoming shipment where an alternative Vietnam-side option could be compared for trucking/port coordination, export customs/documentation and ocean routing.

Large/heavy fabricated cargo may require special equipment, OOG/breakbulk planning, lifting or carrier acceptance. Do not promise handling or quote before dimensions, gross weight, packing/lifting points and shipment terms are known.

## Suggested first-touch angle
Reference the Hai Phong EPE factory and its stated Japan export activity. Ask whether there is one upcoming shipment for which they would value a second logistics benchmark. Request only the minimum shipment facts needed to qualify: destination/port, cargo description, dimensions and gross weight, ready date and Incoterm.

## Contact execution
1. Email `info@rk-eng-vn.com` with a shipment-specific request rather than a rate sheet; treat a bounce as evidence that the catalogue address is stale.
2. If no deliverability evidence is available, use the current official factory phone +84-225-326-0715~8 to ask for the export/logistics person instead of guessing a personal email.

## Guardrails
- Do not claim RK ENGINEERING is looking for a new forwarder.
- Do not invent current Japan destination ports, frequency, carrier, rates, volume or Incoterm.
- Do not assume container suitability for large fabricated cargo.
- For OOG/heavy cargo, confirm exact dimensions/weight, lifting/packing method, equipment availability and carrier/terminal acceptance before quoting.
