## Why this is qualified
AIZAKI's official Vietnam page states an export mix of Japan 58.4%, USA 32.4%, Vietnam 6.6%, and Thailand 2.6%. The same page explicitly describes delivery from its Vietnam production base to Japan and gives indicative transport planning of about one week by air or two to three weeks by sea after processing. This is direct evidence of recurring Vietnam-origin international cargo, especially Japan-bound cargo; it is not an inference from having a factory alone.

## BYJ lane fit
The plant is in Dong Nai, so do not force this prospect into the Hai Phong → Yokohama campaign. First ask for the actual destination, cargo-ready date and shipment profile. BYJ can position as a Vietnam-side second-check for:
- factory pickup / trucking from Dong Nai;
- export customs coordination;
- ocean or air routing to Japan;
- documentation and current freight-option comparison;
- USA/Thailand routings if a real requirement is disclosed.

## Outreach angle
Lead with the fact that BYJ supports Vietnam-origin freight rather than claiming knowledge of AIZAKI's current carrier or shipment schedule. Ask whether the logistics/procurement team has an upcoming Vietnam → Japan shipment where a second routing/freight check would be useful. If yes, request destination, commodity, packages/dimensions, gross weight/CBM, Incoterm and cargo-ready date before quoting.

## Guardrails
- Do not claim AIZAKI is looking for a new forwarder.
- Do not claim shipment frequency, container volume, carrier, port or current freight spend.
- Do not invent an email address. The verified official page used for this asset publishes the Vietnam factory telephone but no public sales/logistics email in the evidence captured for this run.
- Treat the official export percentages as company-published information and re-check before using them in future external-facing copy if materially old.
