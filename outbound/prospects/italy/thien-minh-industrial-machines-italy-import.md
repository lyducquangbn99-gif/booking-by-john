- Do not claim current shipment volume, frequency, active purchase orders, carrier preference, or buying intent.
- Machinery may be oversized or project cargo; never quote from generic FCL/LCL assumptions without dimensions and weight.
- Re-verify the company's public contact details on its official website immediately before outreach.
- Route any actual quote through current BYJ pricing/operations checks; do not promise capacity, transit time, customs treatment, or equipment availability without confirmation.
