## Qualification before quoting

Confirm:
- exact POL and POD;
- FCL/LCL and container/equipment type;
- whether reefer is required and, if so, temperature/set-point;
- container quantity or CBM/weight;
- cargo-ready date;
- Incoterm;
- pickup/loading address;
- export customs and documentation scope;
- any cold-chain, food/health certificate, inspection or destination requirements.

## Guardrails

- Do not assume every shipment is reefer; confirm equipment and temperature first.
- Do not claim savings, guaranteed space, transit time or carrier allocation without a current verified quote.
- Do not present company-published output figures as verified shipment volume.
- Keep first contact focused on one real upcoming shipment and a second benchmark, not replacing the incumbent forwarder.
