# Booking by John — 30-day revenue playbook

## Offer

Lead with one low-friction offer:

> Send the origin, destination, commodity, weight/CBM, ready date and Incoterm. John will review the practical route and clarify which charges are included.

Do not promise a fixed response time, rate saving, transit time or carrier acceptance before the shipment is checked.

## Ideal prospects

Prioritize companies that:

- import from or manufacture in Vietnam;
- move commercial cargo to Italy, Spain, Indonesia or Taiwan;
- have a named logistics, procurement, import/export or supply-chain contact;
- publish a real company website and business email;
- sell furniture, garments, footwear, machinery, food ingredients, consumer goods or industrial components.

Avoid purchased email lists and bulk blasts.

## Daily workflow

1. Research 20 qualified companies.
2. Record company, country, product, contact, source URL and likely route.
3. Send a personalized first message to no more than one or two relevant people per company.
4. Follow up after 3–5 business days.
5. Stop after two unanswered follow-ups.
6. Record replies, quote requests, quoted shipments and bookings.

## Weekly scorecard

Track:

- qualified prospects researched;
- first messages sent;
- follow-ups sent;
- positive replies;
- quote requests;
- quotes issued;
- bookings won;
- estimated gross profit;
- lead source and landing page.

The first optimization target is not traffic. It is the conversion from qualified conversation to complete quote request.
