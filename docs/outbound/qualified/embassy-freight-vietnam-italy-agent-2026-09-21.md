# Qualified overseas-forwarder / agent prospect — Embassy Freight (Vietnam ↔ Italy)

Qualified: 2026-09-21

## Why this prospect fits BYJ

Embassy Freight is a freight forwarder with a current Vietnam operation and an Italy network. Its official site states that it has offices in Vietnam and Italy and that Vietnam–Europe is one of its core trade lanes. This makes it a relevant forwarding-partner prospect for BYJ when we have Vietnam-origin spot rates or need reciprocal destination/origin support.

This qualification does **not** assume that Embassy Freight currently needs a new Vietnam agent, has an active shipment for BYJ, or will accept co-loading/nomination business. Those points must be discovered before quoting.

## Safe outreach angle

Approach as a peer-forwarder / cooperation discovery, not as a generic shipper pitch:

> We handle Vietnam-origin international freight under BYJ Logistics and periodically have spot requirements and rate checks for Italy, including Genoa / La Spezia and other Italian destinations. I noticed Embassy Freight has teams in both Vietnam and Italy. Would your team be open to reciprocal rate/support requests when we have a shipment that fits your network? If yes, I can send shipment-specific POL/POD, commodity, equipment/CBM, ready date and Incoterm for a proper check.

Do not send a blanket rate without a matching shipment or cooperation signal. If they are open, qualify:

1. Which Vietnam ↔ Italy lanes/offices they want partner enquiries on.
2. Import vs export preference and FCL/LCL/air scope.
3. Whether they accept spot co-loading, destination handling or reciprocal agent requests.
4. Correct commercial/rate contact for shipment-specific RFQs.
5. For each live shipment: POL/POD, commodity, equipment or CBM/weight, Incoterm and cargo-ready date.
