## Qualification
AAB is relevant as an overseas-forwarder prospect because it explicitly publishes Vietnam partner coverage and operates Indonesian forwarding offices. This is stronger than a generic Indonesia forwarder lead, but it does not prove a current shipment, current rate need, or dissatisfaction with an existing Vietnam partner.

## Route-matched positioning
Position BYJ Logistics as an additional Vietnam-origin execution / spot / overflow option, not as a replacement for AAB's existing partner network. Useful scopes to test on a real shipment:
- Vietnam factory pickup and origin trucking;
- export customs / origin coordination;
- FCL or LCL spot checks from HCM or Haiphong to Indonesia;
- document handoff and shipment-status coordination.

## First-touch angle
AAB already covers Vietnam, so avoid a generic 'we can ship from Vietnam' introduction. Ask whether their Indonesia team ever needs an independent Vietnam-origin backup/spot check for a live shipment. If yes, request origin/pickup point, destination, commodity, FCL/LCL/air scope, volume/equipment and cargo-ready date before quoting.

## Guardrails
- Do not claim AAB currently needs a Vietnam agent.
- Do not claim any current sailing, rate, capacity, partnership or shipment volume without live verification.
- Do not infer an email pattern from the company domain.
- Quote only after receiving a real shipment scope and rechecking current carrier/local-charge conditions.
