Practical-guidance label: this checklist was verified against official sources on 22 October 2026. It is not a ruling on the HS code, origin, tariff, licence, tax, customs channel or release of any shipment.

An e-Form D can support an Indonesian claim for the ASEAN Trade in Goods Agreement preferential tariff, but the electronic certificate is only one part of the file. The product must qualify as originating, the Indonesian tariff line and applicable ATIGA rate must be correct, the consignment conditions must be met, and the importer must declare matching data in the PIB.

## What the official systems do

Vietnam's Ministry of Industry and Trade says a complete and valid electronic Form D application is processed through eCoSys, with the result notified within six working hours. When approved, the electronic Form D data are sent through Vietnam's National Single Window for transmission to ASEAN member states. That is an administrative service target, not a cargo cut-off or a guarantee that Indonesia will grant preference.

ASEAN states that the e-Form D has the same legal recognition as the paper form and that all ten ASEAN member states have participated in its exchange since 2019. Indonesia Customs lists five tracking states: AS1, AS2, AS3, NOT and REC. REC means the e-C/O is available in the Customs system; it does not by itself prove that the origin, tariff line or declaration has been accepted.

## Seven checks before booking

### 1. Fix the Indonesian HS line and compare the rates

Indonesia's PMK 43/2022 sets ATIGA duties by tariff line under HS/AHTN 2022. The schedule is not a promise that every Vietnam-origin item is duty-free. It also says that the general import duty applies when it is lower than the ATIGA rate. Ask the Indonesian importer or broker to record the proposed Indonesian HS code, the general rate and the ATIGA rate before quoting landed cost.

### 2. Prove origin for the actual product

A shipment from Vietnam is not automatically originating. PMK 81/2022 recognises wholly obtained or produced goods and, for other goods, rules such as regional value content of at least 40% of FOB, a four-digit change in tariff heading, product-specific rules or accumulation. Where a product-specific rule applies, use that rule. Keep the bill of materials, input origins, calculation or production evidence that supports the criterion shown on the Form D.

### 3. Make the commercial file match the origin file

Freeze the exporter, consignee, product description, quantity, gross weight or other unit, invoice number and date, origin criterion, marks and package details before the certificate request. Indonesia Customs may compare the customs declaration and supporting documents with the Form D and, if the goods are inspected, with the physical goods. Minor discrepancies can be accepted in defined cases, but that is not permission to ship a file with avoidable differences.

### 4. Treat transmission status as a booking control

Send the e-Form D reference number and date to the Indonesian importer as soon as issued. Check the INSW tracking record. AS1 to AS3 describe progress through the exporting and importing gateways; NOT means the e-C/O could not be processed and the importing National Single Window should be contacted; REC means it is available in the Customs system. An operationally safer handoff is to resolve NOT or missing data before the importer submits the PIB, while recognising that the official source does not prescribe REC as a carrier booking condition.

### 5. Preserve transit and transshipment evidence

Indonesia Customs says transit through another country can remain compatible with preference when it is for geographical or transport reasons, the goods are not traded or consumed there, and they undergo no production beyond unloading, loading and actions needed to keep them in good condition. For a routed shipment, retain the through transport document, route, container and seal history, transshipment evidence and any Customs-control record that can support those conditions.

### 6. Align the PIB claim

For the preference claim, the importer must correctly enter the ATIGA facility code and the e-Form D number and date in the relevant Indonesian customs declaration. Reconcile those fields with the final invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill and the received e-Form D record. An incorrect reference can disconnect an otherwise valid certificate from the import entry.

### 7. Use PKBSI when origin uncertainty is material

Indonesia Customs describes Penetapan Keasalan Barang Sebelum Impor, or PKBSI, as an optional pre-import origin determination. It must be requested and issued before the PIB is submitted. The determination can be used for three years, but only under its stated facts and for the named applicant; changed origin conditions or inaccurate data can make it unusable or lead to revocation. Consider it for recurring products where origin treatment is genuinely uncertain, not as a compulsory step for every shipment.

## Vietnam freight and import-export impact

The biggest logistics benefit is not the certificate alone; it is moving the tariff and origin decision upstream of cargo cut-off. If the HS line, origin criterion, certificate data and PIB reference are agreed before booking, the exporter can avoid last-minute invoice changes, certificate re-issue requests or a route that is difficult to evidence.

The limits are equally important. None of the sources guarantees an ATIGA rate for a particular product, a green customs channel, acceptance of a discrepancy, a clearance time, a refund, a freight saving or release without inspection. Confidence is high in the cited rules and live system descriptions. Shipment-level confidence remains conditional on classification, the exact product-specific rule, the evidence file, transmission status and Indonesia Customs' review.
