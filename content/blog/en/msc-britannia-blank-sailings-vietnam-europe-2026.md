MSC announced on 14 October 2026 that two Britannia sailings on the Asia-North Europe trade will be blanked: voyage QB645W in week 45 and QB646W in week 46.

This is directly relevant to Vietnam. MSC's official Britannia rotation calls Vung Tau before Singapore and Colombo, then serves Felixstowe, Rotterdam, Zeebrugge, Antwerp, Gdansk, Gdynia and Klaipeda. A booking tied to either voyage therefore needs a live carrier check rather than an assumption that the original sailing plan remains valid.

## What MSC has and has not announced

MSC says the capacity adjustment is due to ongoing market challenges. The carrier also says customers may continue placing bookings as usual because it is arranging contingency plans with alternative services.

The notice does not identify a replacement vessel, replacement voyage, revised Vung Tau departure, new cut-offs, routing, transshipment port, freight rate, surcharge or shipment-level delay. It also does not say that every Vietnam-Europe booking in weeks 45 and 46 is affected. The decisive evidence is the service and voyage shown on the actual booking.

## Five checks for Vietnam-Europe shipments

- Pull every MSC export booking planned for weeks 45 and 46 and identify the booked service and voyage. Flag QB645W and QB646W; do not screen only by destination or estimated departure date.
- Compare the latest booking confirmation with the original shipment plan. Record the port of loading, destination, container type, cargo readiness date and all current cut-offs before asking for a change.
- For a flagged booking, request written confirmation from MSC of the alternative service, voyage, routing, port calls, estimated departure and arrival, and revised CY, shipping-instruction and VGM cut-offs. The general notice alone does not supply those shipment details.
- Do not dispatch a truck, release an empty container or change customs and documentation milestones solely on the basis of the old sailing plan. Reconcile the carrier's written instruction with the terminal, depot, trucker and customs broker.
- Send one controlled update to the shipper, buyer, consignee and production team. Distinguish confirmed carrier facts from estimates, then keep the revised booking confirmation and change log in the shipment file.

## Concrete impact on Vietnam freight and trade

For a Vietnam export actually booked on QB645W or QB646W, the immediate issue is not a proven port closure or a universal Europe capacity shortage. It is that the nominated Britannia sailing will not operate and the shipment may need a carrier-arranged alternative.

That can change the operational sequence for container release, factory loading, trucking, terminal delivery, customs work and documents. The correct response is to obtain the revised shipment instruction first, then re-align each milestone. Importers expecting cargo from Vietnam should use the carrier-confirmed replacement plan before revising inventory or delivery commitments.

Confidence is high that MSC listed these two Britannia voyages as blanked and that Britannia's official rotation includes Vung Tau. Uncertainty remains high for any individual booking until MSC confirms its alternative arrangement. This article does not claim a fixed delay, higher freight rate, surcharge, equipment shortage, rolled cargo, guaranteed space or impact on another carrier.
